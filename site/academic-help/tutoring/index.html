Tutoring

Free drop-in, one-on-one peer tutoring for VCEA courses. No appointment needed.

See today's tutors

Where and when: Bowers Tutoring Center, Schweitzer Engineering Hall (SEH) 130, Monday to Friday, times vary by course (see tutor schedule), Closed when WSU is not holding classes.

How it works

Walk in, no appointment. Tutors are trained Voiland College students who have already passed the course they tutor. Stop by early in the semester to see the space and the tutor schedules posted on the wall.

What to bring

  • Textbook and notes
  • The assignment you are working on
  • A scientific calculator
  • Specific questions, so the tutor knows where you are stuck

Holiday schedule

Tutoring runs only on days WSU holds classes, so it is closed on holidays such as Presidents' Day.
