Academic Advising

Every VCEA student has an assigned advisor (assigned at New Coug Orientation) who helps you plan your degree and stay on track.

Find my advisor

Who is my advisor?

Advisors are assigned at New Coug Orientation. In myWSU: open the Academic Advising tile, then Academic Progress, then My Academics. [VERIFY: current myWSU steps]

Before your appointment

  • Review your Academic Requirements report
  • Bring your questions
  • Check for holds

Reading your Academic Requirements report

  1. Sign in to myWSU with your WSU Network ID
  2. Open the Academic Advising tile
  3. Choose Academic Progress in the left menu
  4. Open the My Academics tab to see your report
