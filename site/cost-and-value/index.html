Why WSU Costs Less

Whether you live in Washington or another state, a year at WSU costs less than at the Seattle flagship or the Oregon land-grant, and WSU engineers start at about the same salaries.

A Washington student pays $4,086 a year less at WSU than in Seattle, and $27,449 less than paying full out-of-state price in Oregon. WUE tuition is 150 percent of in-state tuition, so even with WUE, Oregon costs at least its in-state budget of $34,878: $3,659 more than WSU.

Tuition for Washington residents is nearly the same at WSU and in Seattle. The difference is living costs: food and housing in Pullman cost $4,783 less than in Seattle and $2,764 less than in Oregon.

Total cost includes tuition and fees, books and supplies, food and housing, and other expenses. Your family's price after aid depends on income: try WSU's net price calculator.

Source: College Navigator: WSU, College Navigator: Seattle comparison school, College Navigator: Oregon comparison school, WICHE: Western Undergraduate Exchange, Voiland College scholarships. Published budgets for full-time beginning students, 2024-25; admission rates, fall 2024. Oregon row assumes WUE and WUEE close the tuition gap

Out-of-state? It gets better

WSU's out-of-state tuition and fees are already $13,259 a year below the Seattle flagship's and $8,240 below the Oregon land-grant's (2024-25). Students from Western Undergraduate Exchange states can add the WUE Distinguished Cougar Award and WSU Engineering and Architecture's $2,000-a-year WUEE Scholarship, which together close the gap between in-state and out-of-state tuition. Scholarships from one application

  • Distinguished Dean's Scholarship: full tuition, renewable for up to four years
  • Dean's Scholarship: more than 300 awarded every year to new first-year and transfer students
  • WUEE Scholarship: $2,000 a year for out-of-state engineering and computer science students

One form, the WSU General Scholarship Application, covers all three. Priority deadline: January 31.

Source: Voiland College scholarships

More students get their shot

WSU admits 87 percent of applicants, compared with 39 percent at the Seattle flagship and 77 percent at the Oregon land-grant. At WSU Engineering and Architecture, first-year students start in the college right away, with ENGR 101 and 102 and free tutoring from day one.

Source: College Navigator: WSU, College Navigator: Seattle comparison school, College Navigator: Oregon comparison school, First-Year Program

Earn while you learn

  • ProPEL: earn credit for paid internships and co-ops while staying a full-time student. ProPEL
  • Micro-internships: paid short projects through Parker Dewey, often remote.
  • Campus roles: peer tutor, first-year peer coordinator, Peer Career Guide, Wellness peer supporter. [VERIFY: which roles are paid]

Included with tuition

  • Free drop-in tutoring and academic advising
  • Mentoring and career coaching
  • The FIZ maker space and more than 40 student clubs
  • The Wellness Center

Source: Voiland College Club Hub

Comparing offers? Ask every school

  • What is our net price after grants, not the sticker price?
  • What will housing and living costs be each year?
  • When and how do I get into my major?
  • Do scholarships renew every year?
  • Can I earn credit for a paid internship?
