Why Voiland College Costs Less

Save $16,344 over four years compared with the Seattle flagship, and start at the same salaries in mechanical, civil and electrical engineering.

Plan a visit

$16,344less to attend over four years, Washington residents
$17,763less every year for out-of-state students
87%of applicants admitted, vs. 39% in Seattle

Source: College Navigator: WSU, College Navigator: Seattle comparison school. Published total cost for beginning students living on campus, 2024-25. Four-year savings = 4 x the $4,086 yearly in-state difference. Admission rates, fall 2024

Out-of-state? It gets better

WSU's out-of-state tuition and fees are already $13,259 a year below the Seattle flagship's (2024-25). Students from Western Undergraduate Exchange states can add the WUE Distinguished Cougar Award and Voiland College's $2,000-a-year WUEE Scholarship, which together close the gap between in-state and out-of-state tuition. [VERIFY: amounts for 2027-28]

Source: College Navigator: WSU, College Navigator: Seattle comparison school, Voiland College scholarships, WICHE: Western Undergraduate Exchange

Scholarships from one application

  • Distinguished Dean's Scholarship: full tuition, renewable for up to four years
  • Dean's Scholarship: more than 300 awarded every year to new first-year and transfer students
  • WUEE Scholarship: $2,000 a year for out-of-state engineering and computer science students

One form, the WSU General Scholarship Application, covers all three. Priority deadline: January 31.

Source: Voiland College scholarships

More students get their shot

WSU admits 87 percent of applicants, compared with 39 percent at the Seattle flagship. At Voiland College, first-year students start in the college right away, with ENGR 101 and 102 and free tutoring from day one.

Source: College Navigator: WSU, College Navigator: Seattle comparison school, First-Year Program

Earn while you learn

  • ProPEL: earn credit for paid internships and co-ops while staying a full-time student. ProPEL
  • Micro-internships: paid short projects through Parker Dewey, often remote.
  • Campus roles: peer tutor, first-year peer coordinator, Peer Career Guide, Wellness peer supporter. [VERIFY: which roles are paid]

Included with tuition

  • Free drop-in tutoring and academic advising
  • Mentoring and career coaching
  • The FIZ maker space and more than 40 student clubs
  • The Wellness Center

Source: Voiland College Club Hub

Comparing offers? Ask every school

  • What is our net price after grants, not the sticker price?
  • What will housing and living costs be each year?
  • When and how do I get into my major?
  • Do scholarships renew every year?
  • Can I earn credit for a paid internship?
