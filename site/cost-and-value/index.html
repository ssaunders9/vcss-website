WSU vs. UW: Cost and Value

A year at WSU costs about $5,600 less than a year at UW Seattle, about $22,000 over four years. In mechanical, civil and electrical engineering, WSU graduates earn about the same as UW graduates in their first year.

Out-of-state students

WSU's out-of-state tuition is $13,646 a year less than UW's before any aid. Engineering students from WUE states can also combine the WUE Distinguished Cougar Award with Voiland College's $2,000 WUEE Scholarship, which together close the gap with WSU's in-state tuition. [VERIFY: amounts each year]

Getting in

WSU admits about 87 percent of applicants and UW Seattle about 39 percent. Admission to a specific engineering major works differently at each school; ask both how and when you would enter your major. [VERIFY: current Voiland and UW major admission policies]

What comes with tuition at Voiland College

  • Free drop-in tutoring, advising, mentoring and career coaching
  • ProPEL: credit for paid internships while you stay a full-time student
  • Scholarships from one application, including full-tuition Distinguished Dean's awards
  • The FIZ maker space and 40+ student clubs

Comparing offers? Ask every school

  • What is our net price after grants, not the sticker price?
  • Do the scholarships renew every year, and what does renewal require?
  • When and how do I get into my major, and what if I'm not admitted?
  • What do graduates in my major earn in their first year?
  • Can I earn credit for a paid internship without falling behind?
