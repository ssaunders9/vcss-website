What Voiland College Really Costs

Most families pay far less than the sticker price. At WSU the average net price after grants and scholarships is $13,462 a year, and Voiland College graduates in most majors earn $75,000 to $86,000 in their first year out.

Visit and ask us

Sticker price vs. what families pay

WSU's published cost of attendance for a Washington resident is $27,914 a year, including tuition ($13,888), housing, food and books. After grants and scholarships, families pay much less, and lower-income families pay the least.

Out-of-state students

Out-of-state tuition is $30,994 a year, but most engineering students from western states do not pay it. Students from Western Undergraduate Exchange (WUE) states can combine the WUE Distinguished Cougar Award with Voiland College's WUEE Scholarship, an extra $2,000 a year. Together they close the gap with in-state tuition. [VERIFY: amounts each year]

Scholarships from one application

  • Distinguished Dean's Scholarship: full tuition, renewable for up to four years
  • Dean's Scholarship: more than 300 awarded each year to incoming first-year and transfer students
  • WUEE Scholarship: $2,000 a year for out-of-state engineering and computer science students

One form, the WSU General Scholarship Application, covers all three. Priority deadline: January 31. Scholarship details.

What graduates earn and owe

In every engineering and computing major with data, median first-year earnings are more than three times the median federal loan debt. Typical loan payments are about $200 to $270 a month.

Earn while you learn

  • ProPEL: paid internships and co-ops for academic credit, while you stay a full-time student. ProPEL
  • Micro-internships: paid short projects through Parker Dewey, often remote.
  • Student roles: peer tutor, first-year peer coordinator, Peer Career Guide and Wellness peer supporter. [VERIFY: which roles are paid]

Free support is part of the price

Tutoring, advising, mentoring, career coaching, the FIZ maker space and the Wellness Center are free to every Voiland College student. Staying on track to graduate on time is one of the biggest ways to keep college affordable.

First-Year Program, Tutoring, Career Services.

Comparing offers? Ask every school

  • What is our net price after grants, not the sticker price?
  • Do the scholarships renew every year, and what does renewal require?
  • Can I earn credit for a paid internship without falling behind?
  • What do graduates in my major earn in their first year?
  • What free tutoring and advising come with tuition?
