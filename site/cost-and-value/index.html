Why Voiland College Costs Less

Save about $22,000 over four years compared with the Seattle flagship, and graduate into the same starting salaries in mechanical, civil and electrical engineering.

Lower cost of attendance

Per yearWSU PullmanSeattle flagshipYou save at WSU
Cost of attendance, Washington resident$27,914$33,524$5,610
Out-of-state tuition$30,994$44,640$13,646

Cost of attendance is tuition plus housing, food, books and transportation. Pullman's lower cost of living is the difference, and it adds up every year.

Average net price after grants is about the same at both schools ($13,462 at WSU), so WSU's aid keeps pace while everyday costs stay lower. Your family's number depends on income: run WSU's net price calculator. [TBD: link]

Source: College Scorecard, WSU, College Scorecard, Seattle comparison school

Out-of-state? It gets better

WSU's out-of-state tuition is already $13,646 a year below the Seattle flagship's. Students from Western Undergraduate Exchange states can add the WUE Distinguished Cougar Award and Voiland College's $2,000-a-year WUEE Scholarship, which together close the gap with in-state tuition. [VERIFY: amounts each year]

Source: Voiland College scholarships, WICHE, Western Undergraduate Exchange, College Scorecard

Scholarships from one application

  • Distinguished Dean's Scholarship: full tuition, renewable for up to four years
  • Dean's Scholarship: more than 300 awarded every year to new first-year and transfer students
  • WUEE Scholarship: $2,000 a year for out-of-state engineering and computer science students

One form, the WSU General Scholarship Application, covers all three. Priority deadline: January 31.

Source: Voiland College scholarships

More students get their shot

WSU admits about 87 percent of applicants, compared with about 39 percent at the Seattle flagship. At Voiland College, first-year students start in the college right away, with ENGR 101 and 102, a peer coordinator in every section, and free tutoring from day one.

Source: College Scorecard, WSU, College Scorecard, Seattle comparison school, First-Year Program

Earn while you learn

  • ProPEL: earn credit for paid internships and co-ops while staying a full-time student. ProPEL
  • Micro-internships: paid short projects through Parker Dewey, often remote.
  • Campus roles: peer tutor, first-year peer coordinator, Peer Career Guide, Wellness peer supporter. [VERIFY: which roles are paid]

Included with tuition

  • Free drop-in tutoring and academic advising
  • Mentoring and career coaching
  • The FIZ maker space and 40+ student clubs
  • The Wellness Center

Comparing offers? Ask every school

  • What is our net price after grants, not the sticker price?
  • What will housing and living costs be each year?
  • When and how do I get into my major?
  • Do scholarships renew every year?
  • Can I earn credit for a paid internship?
