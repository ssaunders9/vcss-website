Club Advisors & Officers

Everything a club needs to register, fund itself, promote events, and get mail.

Register or renew as an RSO

WSU Engineering and Architecture clubs register or renew WSU Registered Student Organization status at the start of each fall semester. New clubs should register as soon as possible, because RSO training dates are limited. Not sure of your status? Email Student Engagement Services at getinvolved@wsu.edu.

Funding

Student Engagement Services runs the club funding opportunities. Purchase, food and travel requests go through the college's club purchasing system. [TBD: link and current steps]

Promote your club

Digital signage on WSU Engineering and Architecture monitors
  1. Design the flyer at 16:9 (PowerPoint) proportions.
  2. Add a QR code to a page with dates and details, so one flyer works all term.
  3. Tell us the date to take it down.
  4. Say whether you want it printed for physical flyer boards (up to 20 prints per club).

Email the flyer to vcea.studentclubs@wsu.edu.
