What to do if

Start with your situation. Each one lists the first step and where to go.

I'm behind or failing in a class

Go to tutoring this week, then talk with your instructor. If you are thinking about dropping, see your advisor before the deadline.

Tutoring Academic calendar

I have a registration hold

Open myWSU to see which office placed the hold. Your advisor can help you clear advising holds.

myWSU Academic Advising

I want to drop a class or withdraw

Talk with your advisor first. Dropping can affect financial aid, scholarships and your degree timeline.

Find your advising office Academic calendar

I want to change my major

Meet with an advisor in the major you want. Each school sets its own certification requirements.

Requirements by major

I'm a transfer and my credits didn't count

Contact the transfer coordinator with your transcript and the course you expected to transfer.

Transfer Students

I have a money emergency

Tell us what happened. Emergency aid can help with unexpected costs.

Emergency Aid Scholarships

I'm stressed or overwhelmed

Stop by the Wellness Center to take a break or talk with a peer supporter. In a crisis, call or text 988.

Wellness Center Call or text 988

I need an internship or job

Drop in at SEH 102 or book a career coach. Paid internships can earn credit through ProPEL.

Careers & Internships ProPEL

I don't know who to ask

Email or call the Student Success team and we will point you to the right person.
