Careers & Internships

Career coaching, internships, career fairs, and job search tools for Voiland College students.

Book a career coach

Where and when: SEH Welcome Center, Room 102, 150 SE Spokane St. [VERIFY: Mentoring page still says Dana 140], Peer Career Guide drop-ins: Mon 2–3, Tue 12–1, Wed 3–4, Thu 12–1, Fri 2–3

Who we serve: In-person support for Pullman and Bremerton students; remote support for students at other WSU campuses.

What we do

  • Career coaching and mock interviews
  • Technical career fairs and employer information sessions
  • Networking events and industry tours
  • Job postings and on-campus interviews
  • The ProPEL internship credit program
