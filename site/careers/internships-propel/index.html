Internships & ProPEL

ProPEL lets you earn credit (ENGR 488/489) for a paid internship or co-op.

Apply for ProPEL

How it works

  1. Get an offer. Pay is required, and you need a worksite mentor.
  2. Apply online as soon as you have the offer, and ask your employer to complete the employer form. Both forms are needed.
  3. Enroll in ENGR 488 (or your major's cross-listed 488) while you work, then ENGR 489 the semester after.

Good to know

  • Placements run 12 weeks to 12 months, and many employers prefer six months or longer.
  • You can stay a full-time WSU student while on a full-time internship.
  • Talk to your advisor first about time to degree.
  • How credit counts toward your major varies by department.

Eligibility

  • At least 420 hours (full time up to 12 months, or part time across terms)
  • Paid (wage, stipend, or fellowship)
  • A named mentor/supervisor at the worksite
  • At least 50% related to your major
  • On-site (virtual case by case)
  • Enroll in ENGR 488 (2 cr) during the work term and ENGR 489 (1 cr) afterward

Credit and costs

Tuition applies like any course. Full-time work earns 2 credits of 488, part-time 1. Both 488 and 489 are needed for the tech/free elective. [VERIFY: live page contradicts itself on “one or two credit” and 30-hour thresholds]

Financial aid note

Co-op income earned while enrolled in ProPEL has not counted against your aid calculation. [VERIFY: confirm wording against the current Student Aid Index rules with Financial Aid]

FAQ

Do I need to take other classes?

No. Other WSU courses are not required while you are in ProPEL.

Can I work part time?

Yes. Part-time work can add up to 420 hours over several terms.

Can the internship be remote?

Case by case. On-site is the default.

Do I pay tuition?

Yes, ENGR 488 and 489 are regular courses.

Other experience options

  • Parker Dewey micro-internships: paid projects of 20 to 40 hours over 2 to 4 weeks, often remote, year-round
  • The Forage: free job simulations that take about 5 to 6 hours and give you something to put on a résumé
