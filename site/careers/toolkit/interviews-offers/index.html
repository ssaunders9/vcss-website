Interviews & Offers

From the first screen to the signed offer.

  • Know the format
  • Practice common questions
  • Research the company
  • Send a thank-you note
Types of interviews
  • Screening call, 30 to 45 minutes
  • One-way recorded video
  • Live video or phone: quiet place, charged device, résumé and notes in reach
  • On-site: keep receipts if the company covers travel
  • Panel or peer group: speak to everyone
  • Over a meal: manners count
  • Second interview: longer, more people
Before

Read the job description and prepare an example for each requirement. Learn the organization's mission and recent news. Do a dry run of the route or the video link.

During

Arrive about 15 minutes early. Be honest, emphasize the positive, and when asked about a weakness, name something you are improving. Ask about next steps and do not raise salary first.

What to wear

Dress professionally. The Career Cougar Closet at the Academic Success and Career Center lends professional clothing.
