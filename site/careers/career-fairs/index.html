Career Fairs

Meet employers, practice your pitch, and find your next internship.

What to expect

WSU Career Expos run every fall and spring through the Academic Success and Career Center, and Voiland College hosts a Technical Fair. The Virtual Expo runs on Handshake with group sessions and one-on-one chats with recruiters. It is an introduction, not an interview. Freshmen are welcome and encouraged to come.

Prepare in 5 steps

  1. Update your résumé and get it reviewed in SEH 102
  2. Complete your Handshake profile
  3. Pick the employers you want to meet and read up on them
  4. Practice a 30-second introduction
  5. Plan your outfit

At the fair

  • Arrive early and start with shorter lines.
  • Shake hands, introduce yourself and hand over your résumé.
  • Ask about the application process and collect a contact name from everyone you meet.
  • Be considerate of the students waiting behind you.
  • Skip salary and benefits questions.

Good questions to ask

  • What do you look for in candidates?
  • What training is there for entry-level hires?
  • What assignments would I get?
  • What skills matter most for success here?

After the fair

Send a thank-you note with your résumé attached, keep researching the company, and book a mock interview.

FAQ

How often are fairs held?

Every fall and spring. [VERIFY: Voiland pages say both bi-annual and tri-annual]

What should I wear?

Professional clothing. The Career Cougar Closet at the Academic Success and Career Center can help.

When can I register?

Until the event starts on the day of the event.

What browser works for the virtual fair?

Chrome, with pop-ups allowed.

What if audio or video fails?

Get the recruiter's email so you can send your materials.

Where do I get help at the in-person fair?

Help booths on both floors of the venue. [VERIFY: current venue]
