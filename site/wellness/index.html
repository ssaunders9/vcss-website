Wellness

A free space in Voiland College to relax, recharge, or talk with a trained peer supporter. It is not therapy or long-term counseling.

Where and when: Daggy Hall 101, Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. Closed weekends, university holidays and closures. After hours, call (509) 335-2159 for Counseling and Psychological Services. Right now: Mon to Fri, 9 to 4
In crisis? Call or text 988, call 911, or go to your nearest emergency room. After-hours counseling: (509) 335-2159

Wellness rooms

Relaxation Room

A quiet room for mindfulness, with a massage chair, aromatherapy, relaxation sounds and soft lighting.

Art Room

Drop in and make something. Art supplies are on hand to help you decompress.

Game Room

The largest room, with LED lighting, a TV, floor gaming chairs, tables and a Wii for active games.

How to use the center

  1. Ask a Peer Supporter at the front desk which room is open.
  2. Check in with the QR code at the desk.
  3. Head to your room and settle in.

Peer support conversations

Talk with a trained Peer Supporter or practicum student from psychology, human development and related fields. Good topics: exam or homework stress, time management, lack of sleep, life changes and basic needs.

The center is a place to connect and de-stress. It does not replace counseling.

Become a peer supporter or practicum student

Psychology, social work and similar majors, or anyone who wants to support fellow Cougs, are welcome to apply. [TBD: how to apply]
