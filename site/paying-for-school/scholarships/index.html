Scholarships

One application, the WSU General Scholarship Application, puts you in the running for the Distinguished Dean's, Dean's and WUEE scholarships.

Apply at WSU Scholarships

Where and when: Priority deadline: January 31 for the following academic year. [TBD: confirm year]

Distinguished Dean's Scholarship

The college's top award: full tuition, renewable for up to four years. Finalists come from Dean's Scholarship applicants and are chosen for academic excellence, curiosity, leadership, and a clear sense of purpose.

Dean's Scholarship

More than 300 awarded each year to incoming first-year and transfer students. First-years are considered on high school GPA and test scores. Pullman transfer students need the calculus series and either the calculus-based physics or chemistry series, with a 3.4 GPA or higher. Apply early, since funds are limited.

WUEE Scholarship

Out-of-state engineering and computer science students at WSU Pullman can add $2,000 a year to their Dean's Scholarship for each year they qualify.

School and department scholarships

Open to students certified in a major. Dates and methods vary, so check with your school.
